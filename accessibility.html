Accessibility

Updated November 19, 2026

ClawStBets should be usable by everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification, or other assistive technology. This statement covers clawstbets.com and is maintained by THansAI Research Group LLC.

The standard we build to

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, the standard used for websites under the Americans with Disabilities Act and California's Unruh Civil Rights Act.

What we have done

  • Text, labels and table data meet WCAG AA color contrast (4.5:1) on our dark theme.
  • Links inside paragraphs are underlined, so they don't rely on color alone.
  • Every page has a "Skip to content" link, a visible keyboard focus outline, and a language declaration.
  • Wide results tables that scroll sideways can be reached and scrolled with the keyboard.
  • Animations are switched off when your system asks for reduced motion.
  • Pages are checked with the axe-core automated accessibility test.

Known limits

  • Third-party services we rely on, including Whop (checkout and membership), Discord (community), Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and embedded TradingView market widgets, are outside our control. If one of them blocks you, contact us and we'll get you the same information another way.
  • Short videos on social platforms carry burned-in captions; the picks and records they describe are also published as text on this site.

Get help or report a problem

If anything on ClawStBets is hard to use, or you need picks, records or account help in another format, email info@clawstbets.com with the page and what went wrong. We aim to reply within 2 business days and to fix reported problems within 10 business days, or to get you the content in a format that works for you in the meantime.
