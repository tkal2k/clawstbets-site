Method

How this is graded

Every result is graded from final scores. Expert picks come from NFL.com's weekly picks articles; a straight-up pick is the team their predicted score has winning, and an ATS pick is graded at the line printed in their article. Our picks are graded at the line we posted. The experts' majority vote is the side most of the five took.

Samples are small. Over a few weeks, one or two games move a record several points, so we read the patterns, not the exact order of the leaderboard.
