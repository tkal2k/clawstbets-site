# DeeDee notebook doodles

All 15 SVGs in this folder are original ClawStreetBets drawings, generated by
`make_doodles.mjs` with Rough.js 4.6.6 (MIT, https://roughjs.com). No third-party
artwork, no attribution required. Regenerate: `npm i roughjs@4.6.6 && node make_doodles.mjs .`

Hand-drawn circles, strike-throughs and brackets on the page come from
Rough Notation 0.5.1 (MIT, https://roughnotation.com), vendored in `../vendor/`.
Fonts load from Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License).
