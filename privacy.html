Privacy Policy

Effective June 21, 2026 · Updated November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ClawStBets, operated by THansAI Research Group LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website, Discord community, and related services (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & membership data — when you subscribe through Whop, we receive your email, username, membership status, and transaction confirmations. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.
  • Discord data — if you join our Discord, we process your Discord username/ID and the messages and activity within our server, subject to Discord's own policies.
  • Communications — information you provide when you contact support.
  • Usage & device data — basic, aggregated analytics such as pages visited, referring source, browser type, and approximate region.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and improve the Service and deliver content and picks.
  • To process and manage your membership and billing (via Whop).
  • To communicate with you about your account, updates, and support.
  • To maintain community safety and enforce our Terms.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Payment Processing

Payments are handled by Whop and its payment processors. We never see or store your full card number. Their handling of your payment information is governed by their own privacy policies and security practices.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with: (a) service providers who help us operate the Service (e.g., Whop, Discord, hosting, analytics); (b) authorities or others when required by law or to protect rights and safety; and (c) a successor in the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer.

5. Cookies & Analytics

Our site and providers may use cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality and aggregate analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some functionality.

On clawstbets.com specifically, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless, for aggregate page views. We also run a first-party counter that records which button was clicked, the page, the referring site and which version of a button you were shown. It stores no cookies, IP addresses or account details. That button version is kept in your browser's local storage, and clearing site data removes it.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. We delete or anonymize information when it is no longer needed.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (e.g., the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your personal information, and to opt out of certain sharing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below. We will respond consistent with applicable law.
